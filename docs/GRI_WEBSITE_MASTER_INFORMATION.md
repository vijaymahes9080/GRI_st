## 2. 🏛️ Complete Navigation Taxonomy

### Category A: About GRI
1. **Vision & Mission**: Rural transformation, Gandhian ideals, Nai Talim philosophy.
2. **Profile & Genesis**: Founded in 1956 by Dr. T.S. Soundaram & Dr. G. Ramachandran.
3. **Best Practices**: Life in GRI, Green Campus, Institutional Distinctiveness.
4. **Former Leadership**: Former Chancellors and Former Vice-Chancellors list.
5. **Campus & Emblem**: Campus profile, official emblem/logo significance, location guide, interactive map.

### Category B: Governance & Statutory Bodies
1. **Executive Council (BoM / EC)**: Composition, roles, and university policies.
2. **Academic Council**: Curriculum oversight and degree approvals.
3. **Finance Committee**: Budgeting and financial governance.
4. **Planning & Monitoring Board**: Institutional growth and strategic planning.

### Category C: Administration & Leadership
1. **Chancellor, Vice-Chancellor, Registrar, Controller of Examinations (CoE), Finance Officer, Chief Vigilance Officer**.
2. **Deans of Schools & Heads of Departments (HODs)** directory.

### Category D: Academics & Schools
1. **CBCS System**: Choice Based Credit System regulations (CIA 40% + ESE 60%).
2. **7 Major Schools**:
   - School of Agriculture & Rural Development
   - School of Tamil, Indian Languages & Fine Arts
   - School of Sciences
   - School of Health Sciences & Rural Sanitation
   - School of Management Studies
   - School of Social Sciences
   - School of Engineering & Technology
3. **Special Academic & Research Centres**:
   - Centre for Women's Studies
   - Centre for Geoinformatics
   - Centre for Social Exclusion and Inclusive Policy (CSEIP)
   - Rural Energy Centre (REC)
   - Research & Development Cell (RDC)

### Category E: Admissions 2026-2027
1. **Prospectus 2026-27**: UG, PG, Diploma, Certificate, and Skill-Based Vocational Courses via CUET.
2. **Regulations**: M.Phil, Ph.D. (July 2026 Batch), D.Sc. & D.Litt. Regulations & Application.
3. **Fee Policies**: Hostel Fee Breakdown and Fee Refund Policy.
4. **New Course Offerings**:
   - Generic Electives by Department of Gandhian Thought and Peace Science
   - Multidisciplinary & Value Added Courses by Department of Biology
   - French Language Courses by School of English
   - **ITEP 2026-2027** (Integrated Teacher Education Programme)

### Category F: Examinations & Evaluation
1. **ESE Timetables**: End Semester Exam schedules for UG/PG/B.Voc.
2. **Ph.D. Tracking**: Online Ph.D. Coursework and Viva-Voce status tracker.
3. **Transcripts & Certificates**: Application forms for Official Transcript and Duplicate Certificate.
4. **e-SANAD**: Certificate verification integration.

### Category G: Extension, Outreach & Flagship Schemes
1. **Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA)**: Regional Coordinating Institute for Village Adoption.
2. **Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK)**: Farm advisories and agricultural extension.
3. **DDU-KK**: Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Kaushal Kendra skill training.
4. **MMTTC**: Malaviya Mission Teacher Training Centre.
5. **Viksit Bharat @2047**: Student innovation initiative.
6. **e-Samadhan**: UGC Grievance Redressal Ombudsperson portal.

### Category H: Campus Facilities & Infrastructure
1. **Hostels**: Boys Hostel, Girls Hostel, Working Women Hostel.
2. **Library**: Central Library, OPAC, e-Journals, Dr. G.R. Books Gallery.
3. **Facilities**: Computer Centre, Internet Browsing Centre, Centre for E-Content Development, Physical Education & Yoga, Instrument Facility (NMR, XRD), UBA Seaweed Startup Facility, Museum, Audio Visual Centre, Lecture Capturing System, Animal House, Business Lab, Art Gallery.
4. **Infrastructure**: Health Centre, Canteen, Bank, Day Care Centre, Examination Hall.
